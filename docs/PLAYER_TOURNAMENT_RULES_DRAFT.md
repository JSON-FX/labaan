# Player tournament rules — Wallet economy draft

Status: Product draft for counsel and operations review

## Currency and entry

- Credits and Victory Points are separate, closed-loop units with no cash value.
- Credits may pay a published tournament entry fee. Victory Points cannot pay
  entry fees and cannot be purchased, transferred, withdrawn, or converted.
- A registration is confirmed only after the server records the Credit debit.
  Reservations, redirects, and screenshots are not proof of entry.
- A cancelled registration or tournament returns the exact Credit fee through
  a new wallet transaction. The original transaction is never edited.

## Starting and rewards

- Each tournament publishes its capacity, minimum confirmed teams, start or
  underfill action, format, seeding/bye method, reward rate, cap, and placement
  split before registration opens.
- A tournament may start below capacity when the published minimum is met.
  Otherwise it is postponed or cancelled according to the published rule.
- The final Victory Point pool is calculated from confirmed eligible teams at
  registration lock, plus any verified sponsor allocations, subject to the
  published cap. Entry fees do not create a cash prize.
- Only server-verified results and the locked eligible roster can receive
  Victory Points. Team awards are split using the published deterministic rule.

## Integrity and disputes

- Players must use their own account, follow game and tournament rules, submit
  truthful results/evidence, and avoid cheating, collusion, harassment,
  impersonation, account sharing, automation abuse, and payment abuse.
- Result verification and disputes use Labaan's evidence and moderator process.
  Disqualification, removal, or bans may affect eligibility under the rules
  published for that tournament.
- Labaan may hold registrations, rewards, purchases, or accounts for review
  when fraud, duplicate-account, refund-deficit, or integrity signals appear.

## Shop

- Victory Points may buy only the products shown as available on the supported
  platform. The initial Shop excludes cash, wallet vouchers, gift cards, cash
  equivalents, user resale, and randomized loot boxes.
- An eligible pre-delivery cancellation restores Victory Points through a new
  wallet transaction. Delivered items follow the product's disclosed policy.

These product rules do not replace the final Terms of Service, contest rules,
game-publisher rules, consumer disclosures, or applicable Philippine law.
