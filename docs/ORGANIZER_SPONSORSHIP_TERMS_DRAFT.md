# Organizer sponsorship terms — product draft

Status: Product draft for counsel, PayMongo, and operations review

- Sponsorship is available only to approved organizers through the web Host
  portal for an eligible Wallet tournament draft.
- Package price, Victory Point allocation, purchase limit, tournament cap,
  attribution choice, and cancellation policy are shown before checkout.
- Payment is made to Labaan through an approved provider. A redirect or receipt
  does not activate rewards; only a verified signed provider event does.
- Sponsorship creates a locked tournament allocation. It never credits the
  organizer's personal wallet and cannot be transferred, withdrawn, resold, or
  assigned to a chosen winner.
- Labaan combines the verified sponsor allocation with the published
  entry-scaled and approved platform/brand allocations, without exceeding the
  tournament cap. Labaan awards the locked pool from verified results.
- Before reward lock, an eligible cancellation requests a full refund to the
  original payment method. After reward lock, cancellation or exceptional
  resolution requires manual review under the published tournament policy.
- Organizers must provide accurate identity and attribution information, use
  authorized funds and branding, disclose material sponsor relationships, and
  comply with game-publisher, advertising, tournament, and Philippine law.
- Provider failures, duplicate events, mismatched amounts, refunds, chargebacks,
  and abuse signals may place the order or tournament into manual review.

These are operational product terms, not final legal terms. Production launch
requires written payment-provider approval and Philippine legal review.
